## Key product facts

- A kit is a motor wheel with controller, display, throttle, brake levers, pedal assist sensor, wiring and torque arms. Kits do not include a battery. A bundle is a kit plus a battery and charger.
- Price ranges: kits £230.00 to £520.00; bundles £399.00 to £875.00; batteries £350.00 to £420.00.
- Wheel sizes sold: 26, 27.5 and 29. Displays: SW900 or UKC1. Battery capacity options: 20ah, 25ah, 30ah.
- Battery included in each bundle: 250W is 36V; 500w, 750w and 1000w are 48V; 1500w and 2000w are 52V; 3000w and 5000w are 72V.
- Battery voltage each kit is compatible with: 500w kit 36V and 48V; 1000w kit and 1500w kit 48V and 52V; 2000w kit 52V and 72V; 3000w kit and 5000w kit 72V.
- Fit: rear hub motor. Rear dropout width 135mm, except the 5000W kit and bundle which need 150mm. Axle quick release or bolt on. Freewheel screw on, up to 7 speed. The 72V battery pack is 445mm base x 265mm tall x 90mm wide.
- Fitting: "Most people fit a kit in 60 to 90 minutes." Tools: "Allen keys, a spanner set, tyre levers and cable ties."
- Speed stated by the store: 250W kits assist up to 15.5mph. "1500W does 45 to 55km/h. 3000W does 60 to 80. The 5000W goes past 80." Other kits are not stated.
- Range stated by the store: "As a rough guide a 20ah battery gives 40 to 60 miles of mixed riding." No figure per kit.
- Charging time is not stated anywhere (unclear in store content).
- Stock levels are not tracked in Shopify. Every variant shows as available.

## UK road legality

- Only the ACX 250W Road Legal Bundle is sold as legal on UK public roads (250W, pedal assist, assistance cuts out at 15.5mph, rider 14 or over).
- All kits and bundles above 250W are "sold for private land and off road use only". ACX does not supply registration, type approval or insurance documents for them.
- The FAQ tab on product pages says "250W stickers and speed limiters can be used to comply with local laws", which does not match the UK Road Legal Guide (unclear in store content).

## Warranty and returns

- Warranty: 12 months against manufacturing faults on kits, bundles and batteries. The 250W bundle description says 24 months on its battery pack (unclear in store content).
- Faulty, damaged or wrong items: repair, replacement or refund, and ACX covers return postage. 30 days from delivery to reject a faulty item for a full refund. The customer emails the order number with photos or a short video.
- Change of mind: the Refund policy gives 14 days from receipt, items unused, unfitted and in original packaging, batteries sealed and never charged, return postage paid by the customer. The FAQ page and product pages say 30 days and the home page FAQ says no change of mind returns (unclear in store content).
- Refunds go to the original payment method within 14 days of the goods reaching ACX, usually within 2 to 5 working days.
- Customers must wait for ACX to confirm the return and send the return address before posting anything back.
