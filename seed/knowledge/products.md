# ACX Ebikes product catalogue

Source: ACX Ebikes Shopify store (https://acxebikes.com), exported 2026-11-14. Covers every ACTIVE product published to the online store (35 products). Prices are in GBP (£) and are the store prices on the export date; taxes are included in prices. Text is copied from the store. Where the store contradicts itself the line is marked "(unclear in store content)".

Notes that apply to every product:
- Inventory is not tracked in Shopify for any product, so stock quantities are not known. "Available" below means the variant can be added to the cart.
- No product has an SKU set and no product has metafields (no spec or FAQ metafields exist in the store).
- "Shipping profile" is the Shopify delivery profile the product belongs to. The rates for each profile are in pages.md under "Shipping rates shown at checkout".
- "Compare at" is the crossed out price shown next to the current price.

Known contradictions inside the product pages (unclear in store content):
- Battery bag price: kit and bundle descriptions say "Add battery bag · £35", but the battery bag product and the add-on boxes on the same pages show £45.00.
- Spare battery: descriptions of the 500w, 750w, 1000w, 1500w and 2000w bundles offer "Add spare 72V 20Ah battery · £360" although those bundles include a 48V or 52V battery. Whether a 72V pack can be used with them is not stated.
- Delivery time: the description, the delivery note under the Add to Cart button, the text under the "Warranty" tab and the checkout rate names give different delivery times for the same product. Each is copied below as written.
- Returns: the "Warranty" tab on kit and bundle pages says "30-day return window on unused kits", while the official Refund policy gives 14 days for change of mind returns.
- Warehouse: the 2000w and 3000w kits and bundles say they ship from the UK warehouse (27.5 and 29 inch wheels), while their Shopify shipping profile is named "2000W and 3000W USA warehouse" and offers "FREE USA Shipping (2-3 days)" to the United States.
- Kit pages (no battery included) still show "12-month warranty on motor, controller and battery" under the "Warranty" tab.

#### ACX 250W Road Legal Bundle

The only kit we sell that is legal to ride on UK roads. No licence, no insurance, no tax, no registration, no number plate. Ride it anywhere a normal bicycle is allowed, including cycle lanes and shared paths.

#### Why this one is different

Everything else on this site is built for private land. This one is built to the UK EAPC rules, so it is treated as a bicycle in law.

- 250W continuous rated motor
- Pedal assist, the motor helps while you pedal
- Rider must be 14 or over. That is the only rule
- Legal on roads, cycle lanes and cycle paths across England, Scotland and Wales
