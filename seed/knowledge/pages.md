## UK Road Legal Guide

- URL: https://acxebikes.com/pages/uk-road-legal-guide
- Last updated in Shopify: 2026-10-27

What counts as road legal in the UK

In the UK an electric bike can be ridden on public roads, cycle lanes and cycle paths with no licence, no tax, no insurance and no registration only if it meets the Electrically Assisted Pedal Cycle rules. There are four conditions.

It has working pedals that can propel the bike.

The motor is rated no higher than 250W continuous.

Motor assistance cuts out at 15.5mph (25km/h).

The rider is 14 or over.

A throttle is allowed only as a walk assist up to 3.7mph. A throttle that drives the bike up to full speed without pedalling takes the bike outside the rules.

If it meets all four, it is treated as a bicycle in law. You can ride it anywhere a normal bike can go.

What happens above 250W

Anything rated above 250W, or that assists above 15.5mph, or that runs on throttle alone beyond walking pace, is not an electric bike in law. It is a motor vehicle. To use one on a public road, cycle path, pavement, park or any other public place you would need all of the following.

DVLA registration and a number plate.

Vehicle tax.

Motor vehicle insurance.

A driving licence covering that vehicle class, which normally means at least a CBT certificate.

An approved motorcycle helmet.

L plates unless you hold a full motorcycle licence.

Without those, riding one on a public road means riding uninsured, unlicensed and unregistered. Police can seize the bike and you can be prosecuted.

Where you can ride a high power kit

Private land, with the permission of the landowner. That is it. Private land does not mean a car park, a canal towpath, a park, a bridleway or a field you do not own. If the public can get to it, it is not private land for these purposes.

Which of our products is which

Our 250W pedal assist kits are built to the road legal specification and can be ridden on UK roads.

Our 500W, 750W, 1000W, 1500W, 2000W and higher kits and bundles are sold for private land and off road use only. We do not supply registration, type approval or insurance documents for them and they cannot be made road legal by us.

It is your responsibility to use what you buy lawfully. If you are not sure which you need, email us before you order and we will point you to the right one.

What is changing

A bill is currently going through Parliament that would ban the sale and supply of e-bikes and conversion kits that fall outside the legal class. It has not become law. If it does, we will tell our customers what it means for them. Nothing on this page is legal advice.

Battery safety

Use only the charger supplied with your battery. Do not charge unattended, overnight, or anywhere that would block your way out of a building. Store batteries somewhere cool and dry. Stop using any battery that is damaged, swollen, smells odd or has taken a hard impact, and contact us.

12 month warranty

Every ACX kit, bundle and battery is covered for 12 months from the date of delivery against manufacturing faults. Every kit is wired and tested by us before it is dispatched.

What is covered

Motor failure that is not caused by damage or misuse.

Controller, display and throttle failure.

Wiring looms and connectors that fail in normal use.

Battery cells or BMS that fail in normal use, where the battery has been charged with the charger we supplied.

Parts missing from a new order.

What is not covered

Damage caused during fitting, assembly, testing or riding.

Damage from incorrect fitting or use outside the intended purpose.

Water damage from immersion, pressure washing or riding through deep water.

Normal wear on consumable parts such as brake pads, tyres, chains, cables and grips.

Cosmetic marks once the kit has been fitted and ridden.

Any product that has been opened, modified, or had its controller reprogrammed.

Batteries charged with a charger we did not supply, left flat for long periods, or physically damaged.

How to make a claim

Email us with your order number, a clear description of the fault, and photos or a short video showing it. Include a photo of the whole installation if the fault involves the motor or wiring.

We diagnose it with you first. Most faults turn out to be a connector, a brake cut off sensor or a display setting and get fixed the same day over email. If the part is genuinely faulty we send a replacement free of charge, or ask you to send the part back with postage paid by us.

We aim to reply to every warranty email within 24 hours.

Your statutory rights

This warranty is in addition to your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and does not replace them. Nothing here limits your right to a repair, replacement or refund for goods that are faulty, not as described or not fit for purpose.

## Battery Safety and Certification

Our batteries are independently tested

Every ACX battery pack is tested to UN38.3, the United Nations standard every lithium battery must pass before it can be transported. Testing is done by an independent laboratory, not by us and not by the factory.

Our 72V 20Ah pack (1440Wh)

Test laboratory: Shenzhen NCT Testing Technology Co., Ltd.

Test report number: NCT26030958XB1 1

Date of issue: 30 July 2026

Standard: UN Manual of Tests and Criteria, ST/SG/AC.10/11/Rev.8/Amend.1, Section 38.3

All eight required tests were passed.

T1 Altitude simulation: pass

T2 Thermal test: pass

T3 Vibration: pass

T4 Shock: pass

T5 External short circuit: pass

T6 Impact and crush: pass

T7 Overcharge: pass

T8 Forced discharge: pass

We also hold the Safety Data Sheet and the sea transport appraisal for this pack. If you need a copy for insurance, a workplace risk assessment or a shipping agent, email us and we will send it.

How to charge and store safely

Use only the charger supplied with your battery. A charger from another pack can deliver the wrong voltage or current and is the most common cause of battery fires.

Do not charge unattended and do not charge overnight while you are asleep.

Do not charge in a hallway, doorway or stairwell, or anywhere that would block your way out of a building.

Charge on a hard surface, not on a bed, sofa or carpet.

Store the battery somewhere cool and dry, out of direct sunlight and away from anything flammable.

Do not let the battery sit completely flat for weeks. Top it up every month or so if the bike is not in use.

Stop using the battery and contact us if it is swollen, cracked, leaking, unusually hot, smells odd, or has taken a hard impact. Do not charge a damaged battery.

Never open a battery pack or attempt to repair it yourself.

Disposal

Lithium batteries must not go in household waste or general recycling. Take an old pack to a local household waste recycling centre that accepts batteries, or contact us and we will point you to the nearest option.

Shipping

Lithium batteries are classed as dangerous goods for transport. That is why some international orders ship without a battery, and why there are a few destinations we cannot send batteries to at all. Email us before ordering from outside the UK and we will confirm what we can send to your address.
