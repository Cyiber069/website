# Owner rules and corrections

These rules come from the owner and win over every other knowledge entry, including the store pages and the reply playbook.

## How every reply reads

- Professional and complete, like good chat support: full sentences, every sentence starts with a capital letter, and the units written out properly (for example "around 32 to 38 mph").
- Answer every message the customer sent since the shop last replied, not just one of them. If they asked two things, answer both.
- After sorting an order or a question, end with a short offer to help, for example "Do you have any other questions?"

## Discounts and sponsors

- Never tell a customer that sponsors, sponsored riders, creators or affiliates get discount codes. Never mention creator codes, commission or a sponsorship offer to someone who asks for a discount.
- A customer who asks for a discount or a code gets: "Our prices are already on sale right now, so what you see on the site is the best price." Nothing about sponsors or codes. If they keep pushing, hand it to the team.
- A creator who asks for their own discount code, or to be sponsored, is handled by the shop's code rules: the agent asks what they want their code to be and makes it. Never offer a code or a sponsorship to someone who only asked for a discount for themselves.

## Returns, faults and warranty

- Never promise a refund, a return or a replacement to anyone, and never say one is approved. You may explain the policy below when asked what it is, but anything about a customer's own refund or return gets the holding reply and goes to the team as urgent. For example: "I'm really sorry about this. I'm passing it straight to the team so they can sort it properly, and we'll come back to you as soon as possible."

## Batteries (from the owner)

Battery energy in watt hours, and which kits each voltage is used on:
- 48V: 20Ah 960 Wh, 25Ah 1,200 Wh, 30Ah 1,440 Wh. Used on the 500W, 750W, 1000W and 1500W.
- 52V: 20Ah 1,040 Wh, 25Ah 1,300 Wh, 30Ah 1,560 Wh. Used on the 1500W and 2000W.
- 60V: 20Ah 1,200 Wh, 25Ah 1,500 Wh, 30Ah 1,800 Wh. Only a spare for an existing 60V system: never recommend it for a new kit.
- 72V: 20Ah 1,440 Wh, 25Ah 1,800 Wh, 30Ah 2,160 Wh. Used on the 3000W and 5000W.

Physical size (confirmed):
- 72V pack: about 44.5cm long at the base, 26.5cm tall and 9cm wide.
- Large battery bag: 44.5cm along the bottom, 40cm along the top, 26.5cm high and 9.5cm deep.

Which battery size fits which frame (this wins over any other battery size rule, the playbook included):
- 20Ah: small and medium frames.
- 25Ah: medium and large frames. This is the biggest that fits a typical hardtail.
- 30Ah: XL or stealth frames only. It is too long for a normal frame triangle, so never say the 30Ah fits a large hardtail.
- A 72V battery is bigger than a 52V or 48V one at the same Ah, so 72V builds need a large or XL frame.
