# Owner rules and corrections

These rules come from the owner and win over every other knowledge entry, including the store pages and the reply playbook.

## Discounts and sponsors

- Never tell a customer that sponsors, sponsored riders, creators or affiliates get discount codes. Never mention creator codes, commission or a sponsorship offer to someone who asks for a discount.
- A customer who asks for a discount or a code gets: "Our prices are already on sale right now, so what you see on the site is the best price." Nothing about sponsors or codes. If they keep pushing, hand it to the team.
- A customer who asks to be sponsored or to work with us: "Love that, send me your TikTok or Instagram and I'll pass it to the team." Nothing about discounts, codes, money or commission. The owner makes every code by hand.

## Prices and links (the site wins)

- Bundle starting prices: 500W and 750W from £439, 1000W from £449. Do not give a euro figure from memory: use the exchange rate table when there is one, and say checkout shows the exact price in their currency.
- The bundle usually works out cheaper than the kit plus a battery, but never say that for the 5000W.
- Say "was £X" only when the store shows a crossed out price for that product.
- Never send the link to the £520 "ACX 1500w kit" listing (acx-1500w-kit). The real 1500W kit is acx-1500w-kit-basic at £270.
- Other accessories we sell: ACX seat £27, Surron seat £40, CNC foot pegs £45, fork extensions £40 (ODI plate £15 extra), front number plate £39.99, Pro Taper handlebars £75, downhill forks £190, Baja light £55, mudguard £15. Battery bag £45 in small or large.

## Delivery

- The 2000W and 3000W ship from UK stock in about 3 working days only on 27.5 and 29 inch wheels. On 26 inch they come from the factory in about 2 to 3 weeks.
- Free delivery to England, Scotland and Wales. Northern Ireland is £25 standard or £130 express.
- USA: the 2000W and 3000W are free from our US warehouse in 2 to 3 days. Everything else to the USA is free standard (around 45 days) or £80 express (2 to 4 weeks).
- Australia: £15 air (3 to 5 days) or free standard (30 to 45 days).
- Rest of the world, Switzerland, Canada and Ireland: express takes 2 to 4 weeks, free standard 40 to 70 days (Switzerland £120, Canada £100, Ireland £15 standard or £80 express).
- EU: free express to most EU countries. Portugal, Czechia, Hungary, Latvia, Malta and Bulgaria have no rate yet: hand those to the team.
- Some international orders ship without a battery. For a bundle or a battery going outside the UK, the team confirms what can ship before they order.
- Accessories ordered on their own ship for £4.99.
- We do not ship at weekends or on UK bank holidays.

## Returns, faults and warranty

- Change of mind: return within 14 days of delivery if it is unused and unfitted, in its original packaging, with all parts. Batteries must be unused, sealed and never charged or connected. Return postage is paid by the customer.
- Faulty, damaged or wrong item: we repair, replace or refund and we cover the return postage. Ask for a photo or video and the order number, then hand it to the team.
- Warranty: 12 months against manufacturing faults on the motor, controller and battery.

## Products

- Kit battery voltages: 500W kit 36V or 48V, 1000W and 1500W kits 48V or 52V, 2000W kit 52V or 72V, 3000W and 5000W kits 72V. The speeds in the playbook are with the bundle's battery.
- 5000W: needs a 150mm rear end (stealth or C41 style frame), a strong frame and good hydraulic disc brakes.
- 250W Road Legal: pedal assist only, no throttle, and the assist cuts out at 15.5 mph (25 km/h).
- Every kit and bundle comes with a quick start fitting guide.
- Fitting takes most people 60 to 90 minutes with Allen keys, a spanner set, tyre levers and cable ties.
- Payment: Klarna (up to 24 months) and Shop Pay instalments, for buyers 18 or over, subject to status.
