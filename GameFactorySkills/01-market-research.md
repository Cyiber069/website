# Market Research

> Find what Roblox players care about this week and pick one game structure worth copying.

## When to use

- Before starting any new game.
- When choosing between several candidate ideas.
- When a brief says "make something like X".

## Rules

1. Start from a proven genre: role-playing, simulator, obby or survival.
   Why: a few build days leave no room to test an unproven genre. [Roblox docs: get-started/strategies]
2. Search YouTube for Roblox topics, limited to the last week or month and sorted by views.
   Why: recent high-view videos are a quick proxy for player interest. [repo: ZeroPointRepo/youtube-skills]
3. For a trusted Roblox channel, compare its most-viewed uploads with its latest ones.
   Why: it shows which game types beat that channel's normal numbers. [repo: ZeroPointRepo/youtube-skills]
4. Filter by view count and upload date before pulling any transcript or frame.
   Why: metadata is cheap; transcripts and frames are the costly step. [repo: yt-dlp/yt-dlp]
5. Play each candidate and write down why players join and why they stay.
   Why: that reveals what players value, not just how the game looks. [Roblox docs: get-started/strategies]
6. Read its in-game chat or community server, and watch community videos when time is short.
   Why: these are Roblox's other recommended audience checks. [Roblox docs: get-started/strategies]
7. Check that the structure supports friends, repeat visits and short mobile sessions.
   Why: recommendations reward return days, co-play days and spend days, and most users are on mobile. [Roblox docs: discovery; design-for-roblox]
8. Record eight items per candidate: first 30 seconds, core loop, progression ladder, social hook, monetization fit, update cadence, mobile control risks, security-sensitive systems.
   Why: it forces copying the structure, not the game. [repo: AshExplained/roblox-skills]
9. Add one differentiating twist and an MVP feature list.
   Why: it forces copying the structure, not the game. [repo: AshExplained/roblox-skills]
10. Write a one-page brief naming two or three comparables and an explicit out-of-scope list.
    Why: later feature requests can be checked against it. [repo: m4bwav/roblox-agent]
11. Score each idea 1-5 on first-session clarity, loop repeatability, social retention, progression depth, mobile fit, fair monetization, live-ops potential and build feasibility.
    Why: a repeatable go/no-go filter. [repo: AshExplained/roblox-skills]
12. Reject or rework any idea that scores low on first-session clarity or mobile fit.
    Why: a fast mobile game cannot recover from those two later.
    Starting assumption (not from a source): "low" means 2 or below. [repo: AshExplained/roblox-skills]

## Checklist

- [ ] Genre is one of the four proven ones
- [ ] Recent high-view video list saved, filtered before transcripts
- [ ] One trusted channel checked: popular vs latest
- [ ] Top candidates played; join and stay reasons written
- [ ] Chat or community read for each candidate
- [ ] Friends, repeat visits and short mobile sessions confirmed
- [ ] Eight-item structure sheet filled in
- [ ] Twist and MVP feature list written
- [ ] One-page brief with comparables and out-of-scope list
- [ ] Eight-axis score recorded; veto axes passed
