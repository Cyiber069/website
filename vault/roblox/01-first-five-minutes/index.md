# 01 First Five Minutes

> This chapter covers what a brand-new player sees, does and earns between joining and the five-minute mark, where most players decide to stay or leave.

## Why this chapter exists

A new player arrives with no investment. They tapped a thumbnail, and another experience is one tap away. The opening minutes therefore have one job: let the player perform the core action, feel rewarded, and see something worth working toward, before their patience runs out. Nothing in this chapter requires extra content; it is about ordering and staging what the game already has.

## How to use it

1. Write the opening as a timeline: second 0, 10, 30, 60, 180, 300. For each mark, write what the player is doing and what they just received.
2. Build the first thirty seconds first and test it on phone emulation.
3. Instrument each step as a funnel so drop-off can be seen after launch; see [[Launch Metrics To Measure]].

## Common failures this chapter prevents

- A loading screen followed by a wall of text.
- A spawn point facing a wall or an empty field.
- A first purchase that takes minutes of grinding.
- A purchase prompt before the player has had any fun.
