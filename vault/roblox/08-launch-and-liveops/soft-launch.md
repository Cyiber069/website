# Soft Launch And Store Page

> Publish quietly to a small audience first, fix what the first real players reveal, and prepare an honest, readable experience page before driving any traffic.

## Why soft launch

The first players to see a game should be few, because the first version always has problems testers missed. Promotion spent on a leaky opening is wasted: those players leave and rarely return. A quiet release lets you read the funnel, fix the largest drop and confirm saving works under real conditions.

## Before making the experience public

- The full checklist in [[Playtest Checklist For PC And Phone]] passes.
- Saving has been tested with server shutdowns and rejoining.
- Analytics events for the onboarding funnel are in place; see [[Launch Metrics To Measure]].
- Experience settings are complete: supported devices enabled, maximum players per server chosen to suit the map, and the experience questionnaire for content maturity answered accurately. Why: wrong device settings hide the game from the audience; inaccurate content labels risk moderation.
- A way to shut down old servers and move players to a new version has been tried once.

## The experience page

- **Icon.** One clear subject, high contrast, readable at thumbnail size on a phone. Why: on a crowded home screen the icon is the whole pitch.
- **Thumbnails.** Show actual gameplay and the fantasy of progress, such as the late-game version of the starter tool. Why: accuracy sets expectations; players who arrive expecting something else leave at once.
- **Title.** Short, says what the player does.
- **Description.** First line states the loop; then a brief list of features and the latest update notes.
- **No misleading claims.** Do not promise free Robux, unreleased features or content that is not in the game. Why: it breaks platform rules and disappoints the players it attracts.

## Running the soft launch

1. Make it public without promotion, or share with a small group.
2. Watch the onboarding funnel and error logs daily.
3. Fix the biggest drop-off, publish, and compare the next cohort of new players.
4. Repeat until new players reliably reach the first rebirth-scale goal, then consider promotion.

Why new cohorts: a fix can only be judged on players who joined after it shipped.

## When to promote

Promote when the opening holds players and day-one return is steady across several cohorts. Until then, more traffic only produces more departures. When you do promote, keep the same creative honest to the gameplay, and be ready with an update soon after; see [[Update Cadence]].

## Check before you hand off

- Device support, server size and content questionnaire are set and recorded.
- Icon and thumbnails show real gameplay and read clearly at small size.
- The description's first line states the core loop.
- Funnel events are firing in a test session.
- A plan exists for what will be checked each day of the soft launch.
