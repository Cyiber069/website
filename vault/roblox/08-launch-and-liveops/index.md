# 08 Launch and LiveOps

> This chapter covers releasing quietly first, what to measure once real players arrive, and how to keep a steady rhythm of updates afterwards.

## Why this chapter exists

Launch is the start of the work that matters for retention, not the end. Real players behave differently from testers, and only live numbers reveal where they leave. A game that measures the right few things and responds on a predictable schedule improves steadily; a game that launches loudly without measurement spends its attention on players who meet the worst version.

## How to use it

1. Complete [[Playtest Checklist For PC And Phone]].
2. Soft launch and read the onboarding funnel before anything else.
3. Fix the biggest drop, then re-measure.
4. Promote only once the opening holds players.
5. Settle into a regular update rhythm.

## Principles

- Measure a few things well rather than everything badly.
- Change one significant thing per release so effects can be attributed.
- Never ship an update that risks player data without a tested migration path.
- Communicate with players in the places they already look: the experience description, in-game update notes and the community's social channels.
