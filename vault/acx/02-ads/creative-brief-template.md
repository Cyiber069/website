# Creative Brief Template

> A creative brief states the single message, the audience, the verified facts that may be used, the required format and the claims that must not appear, so creative can be made right the first time.

## Why brief in writing

Creative made from a loose request reflects the maker's assumptions. A written brief makes the intent checkable, keeps unverified claims out at the source, and lets a reviewer judge the result against something other than taste. It also records what was tested, which matters when reading results later.

## The template

```text
CREATIVE BRIEF
Date:                 Requested by:              For test plan:

1. OBJECTIVE
   What this creative must get the viewer to do:

2. AUDIENCE
   Who, and what they already know or believe:   [confirmed / hypothesis]

3. SINGLE MESSAGE
   The one idea to remember, in one sentence:

4. ANGLE AND HOOK
   The angle being tested:
   Hook options for the opening:

5. FACTS ALLOWED
   Copied from the Store Facts Sheet, with sources:
   -
   -

6. MUST INCLUDE
   Product name as written on the store, call to action, destination link,
   any required disclaimer wording:

7. MUST NOT INCLUDE
   Unverified range, speed, safety or legality claims; superlatives;
   competitor names; invented reviews; fake urgency; riding shown in ways
   the brand has not approved:

8. FORMAT
   Platform and placement:      Aspect ratio and length:
   Captions required: yes       Variations needed:

9. ASSETS
   Approved footage, photos, logos and where they are stored:
   Usage rights confirmed for each: yes / no

10. VOICE
    Voice guide attached: yes / no

11. REVIEW
    Reviewer:                   Deadline:
```

## Rules for writing briefs

- **One message per brief.** Why: an ad that says three things is remembered for none.
- **Only facts listed in section 5 may appear.** If the maker needs another fact, it must be verified and added first. See [[Verified Claims Policy]].
- **Show the product honestly.** Use real product imagery; do not depict capabilities or accessories that are not part of the offer.
- **Depict responsible riding.** Follow the brand's guidance on safety gear and riding conduct in visuals; if no guidance exists, ask before producing.
- **Confirm rights** for every image, clip, track and face.
- **Name the variable.** If this brief is version B of a test, state what differs from version A and keep everything else the same; see [[Ad Test Structure]].

## Reviewing delivered creative

Check against the brief line by line: single message present, facts match sources, exclusions respected, format correct, captions readable on a phone with sound off. Video openings should follow [[Short-Video Hook Structure]]. Tone should match the guide from [[Brand Voice Guide Process]].

## Facts to confirm from the store

- Exact product name, current price and promotion for the advertised item.
- Features and figures permitted in section 5.
- Destination page and that it is live.
- From the owner: approved assets, usage rights, and visual safety guidance.

## Check before you hand off

- All eleven sections are completed or marked open.
- Section 5 facts each have a source and date.
- Section 7 exclusions are listed explicitly.
- Asset rights are confirmed.
- A reviewer is named.
