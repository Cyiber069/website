# Weekly Ads Reporting

> Each week, report what was spent and what happened using numbers taken directly from the ad account and store, then separately state what you think it means and what you recommend.

## Why weekly and why this shape

A week covers weekday and weekend behaviour and gives tests time to gather data, while still being frequent enough to stop waste. Separating facts from interpretation matters because a reader should be able to disagree with the conclusion while trusting the numbers. Reports that blend the two tend to drift toward good news.

## Routine

1. Fix the date range, time zone and currency, the same each week.
2. Pull figures directly from the ad platform and the store's order data. Do not retype from memory or estimate.
3. Note where the platform and store disagree and report both. Attribution differs between systems; do not silently pick the kinder one.
4. Fill the template.
5. Have a human review recommendations before any account change.

## Template

```text
WEEKLY ADS REPORT - [date range, time zone]
Data pulled on:          Sources:

1. SUMMARY (three lines at most)

2. NUMBERS (this week | previous week)
   Spend:
   Primary metric from the test plan:
   Supporting metrics:
   Store-side orders and revenue attributed by the store's own data:
   Differences between platform and store figures:

3. TESTS
   Test name | hypothesis | status | data so far vs minimum agreed |
   decision by the pre-set rule, or "not yet enough data"

4. WHAT CHANGED THIS WEEK
   Launches, pauses, budget changes, store changes, stock or price changes,
   tracking issues, outside events the owner mentioned

5. INTERPRETATION (clearly labelled as opinion)

6. RECOMMENDATIONS
   Action | reason | needs approval from

7. OPEN QUESTIONS AND DATA PROBLEMS
```

## Rules

- **Report bad weeks plainly.** Why: decisions made on softened reports cost more later.
- **No conclusions before the agreed minimum data.** Say "inconclusive" rather than naming a leader; see [[Ad Test Structure]].
- **Compare like with like.** Same range length, and note anything that makes the weeks differ, such as a promotion or stock-out.
- **Do not forecast results.** State what happened and what you would test next.
- **Record changes to the store.** A landing page edit can move results as much as an ad; see [[Product Page Checklist]].
- **Include lead quality when ads drive chats.** Ask the messaging side how many conversations were genuine enquiries, using tags from [[WhatsApp Lead Reply Flow]].
- **Never include customers' personal details** in the report.

## Facts to confirm from the store

- Order and revenue figures for the period from the store's own reporting.
- Any price, stock, promotion or page changes during the week.
- Whether tracking was working for the whole period.
- From the owner: the metrics they want to see and the reporting day.

## Check before you hand off

- Date range, time zone and data sources are stated.
- Every number was pulled from a system, not estimated.
- Platform and store discrepancies are shown.
- Interpretation is separated from facts.
- Recommendations name who must approve them.
