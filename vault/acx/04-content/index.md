# 04 Content

> This chapter gives the process for structuring short videos and planning a sustainable content calendar, using only verified product facts and approved footage.

## Why this chapter exists

Organic content lets people see the product in use, answers questions before they are asked, and supplies material that ads can later reuse. It works when it is consistent and useful, and it harms the brand when it exaggerates or shows something the product or the law does not support. Because short video is quick to produce and widely seen, it is also where a careless claim about range, speed or where a bike may be ridden spreads fastest. Process keeps output regular and accurate.

## Notes in this chapter

- [[Short-Video Hook Structure]] breaks a short video into hook, payoff, proof and call to action, with rules for the opening seconds and for honest demonstration.
- [[Content Calendar Process]] explains how to choose content themes from real customer questions, plan a realistic posting rhythm, and review what worked.

## How to use it

1. Read the [[Store Facts Sheet]] and the brand voice guide.
2. Collect real customer questions from chat and comments.
3. Plan a few weeks of topics with the calendar process.
4. Script each video with the hook structure.
5. Submit for human review before posting; agents do not publish to brand accounts without authorisation.

## Guardrails for agents

- Every on-screen or spoken fact follows the [[Verified Claims Policy]].
- Show riding only in ways the brand has approved; when unsure, ask.
- Use only footage, music and likenesses with confirmed rights.
- Do not imitate a real customer or invent a testimonial.
- Label advertising or sponsored content as the platform and local rules require.
- Do not promise results or quote view or sales expectations.
