# Content Calendar Process

> Plan content in short cycles around a few recurring themes drawn from real customer questions, at a rhythm the team can sustain, and review each cycle before planning the next.

## Why plan this way

Posting only when inspiration strikes produces bursts and gaps, and topics chosen by guesswork miss what buyers actually want to know. A light calendar built on real questions keeps output steady, spreads effort, and makes it possible to see which themes earn attention and enquiries.

## Steps

1. **Gather inputs.** Customer questions from chat and comments, objections logged by the messaging team, the [[Store Facts Sheet]], available footage, and dates the owner says matter, such as real promotions or stock arrivals.
2. **Choose three to five themes.** Typical shapes for a considered purchase: answering questions, showing the product in use, explaining how buying and delivery work, care and maintenance, and the people behind the brand. Pick those the brand can support with real material.
3. **Set a rhythm with the owner.** Choose a frequency that can be kept with review time included. Why: consistency over months matters more than volume in one week.
4. **Fill a two-to-four-week plan.** One row per piece.
5. **Produce in batches** using [[Short-Video Hook Structure]].
6. **Review before posting.** A human approves each piece.
7. **Review the cycle.** Note what was posted, how each piece did on the platform's own analytics, and what questions it generated. Carry lessons forward.

## Calendar template

```text
CONTENT CALENDAR - cycle [dates]

Date | Platform | Theme | Topic (source question) | Format | Hook type |
Facts needed + source | Assets + rights | Owner | Status | Reviewer

Status values: idea / scripted / in review / approved / posted
```

## Tracker for review

```text
Posted piece | Theme | Hook type | Platform figures as reported |
Questions or enquiries it prompted | Keep / change / drop
```

## Rules

- **Every topic traces to a real question or a real event.** Why: it guarantees relevance and keeps invented claims out.
- **Every piece lists its facts and sources before scripting.** Follow the [[Verified Claims Policy]].
- **Reuse deliberately.** One shoot can yield several pieces; a strong organic piece can become an ad through the [[Creative Brief Template]].
- **Promotions must be real** with real dates, confirmed at posting time.

## Facts to confirm from the store

- Products currently available and any upcoming changes the owner wants covered.
- Real promotion dates and terms.
- Facts needed for each planned topic.
- From the owner: platforms in use, posting rhythm, reviewers, and available footage with rights.

## Check before you hand off

- Each calendar row has a source question or event.
- Facts and sources are listed for every piece.
- A reviewer is named for every piece.
- The rhythm was agreed with the owner.
- The previous cycle's review is attached or noted as the first cycle.
