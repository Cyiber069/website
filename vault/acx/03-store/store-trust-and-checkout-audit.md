# Store Trust And Checkout Audit

> Follow the path from product page to order confirmation and check that the store looks accountable, states its terms plainly and lets a buyer pay without surprises.

## Why audit the whole path

A buyer of a high-value item looks for signs that a real, reachable business stands behind it, and abandons when costs or steps appear unexpectedly. These problems sit outside any single product page, so they are missed unless someone walks the whole path deliberately.

## How to run it

Walk the path on a phone-sized screen: home page, a product, cart, checkout up to the payment step. Stop before paying unless the owner has requested a test order. Record each line as pass, fail or not applicable.

## Trust signals

- Business name and contact details are easy to find.
- Delivery, returns, warranty, privacy and terms pages exist, are readable and are consistent with one another and with product pages.
- Reviews and ratings come from real customers and are not selectively edited.
- Badges, certifications and partner logos shown are ones the brand actually holds; flag any you cannot verify.
- No fake countdown timers, invented stock warnings or fabricated purchase notifications. Why: they are deceptive and damage trust when noticed.

## Cart and checkout

- The cart shows item, variant, quantity, price and image clearly.
- Delivery cost and estimated time appear before the final step.
- Taxes or duties are shown or explained before payment.
- Form fields are few, labelled, and use the right mobile keyboards.
- Payment options the brand offers are shown accurately; any instalment option states its provider's terms as published.

## Reporting

List failures with evidence and proposed fixes, ordered by buyer impact. Inconsistencies between policy pages and product pages are accuracy issues under the [[Verified Claims Policy]] and go first. Feed confirmed policy text into the [[Store Facts Sheet]] so that replies in [[WhatsApp Lead Reply Flow]] match the site. Page-level checks are in [[Product Page Checklist]].

## Facts to confirm from the store

- Full text of delivery, return, warranty and privacy policies.
- Payment methods and any instalment providers.
- Delivery areas, costs and times.
- Which certifications or badges the brand is entitled to display.

## Check before you hand off

- The path was walked on mobile to the payment step.
- Every line has a result with evidence for failures.
- Policy inconsistencies are flagged first.
- No order was placed and no setting changed without permission.
- Unverifiable badges or claims are listed for the owner.
