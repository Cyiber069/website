# Product Page Checklist

> Review each product page as a first-time phone visitor would, checking that it says what the product is, answers the obvious questions with verified facts, and makes the next step easy.

## Why a checklist

Page problems are individually small, a missing size guide, a buried delivery time, an image that does not show scale, and collectively decisive. A fixed list makes reviews repeatable across products and reviewers, and turns "the page feels weak" into specific fixes.

## The checklist

**First screen**

- Product name matches the name used in ads and the [[Store Facts Sheet]].
- It is clear what the product is and who it suits, without scrolling.
- Price, currency and any promotion with its real end date are visible.
- The buy or add-to-cart button is visible and works with a thumb.

**Media**

- Photos show the product from several angles, key details, and scale with a person.
- Depictions of riding follow the brand's guidance.

**Information**

- Specifications appear in a scannable table with units.
- Every specification matches the brand's source document.
- Range, speed and similar figures carry the conditions the brand publishes with them; nothing is stated more strongly than the source. See [[Verified Claims Policy]].
- Sizing or fit guidance is present.
- What is included, and what is not, is listed.
- Assembly needs and support are explained.

**Reassurance**

- Delivery area, cost and time are stated on or linked from the page.
- Return and warranty summaries link to the full policy.
- Reviews, if shown, are genuine and unedited.

**Function**

- Add to cart works for every variant.
- No broken links, placeholder text or spelling errors.
- Page content matches what current ads promise; see [[Creative Brief Template]].

## Reporting findings

List each failure with a link or screenshot, why it matters to the buyer, and a proposed fix. Order by likely impact: accuracy errors first, then blockers to buying, then missing information, then polish. Do not publish changes without authorisation. Continue with [[Store Trust And Checkout Audit]].

## Facts to confirm from the store

- The authoritative specification source for each model.
- Current price, promotion, stock and lead time.
- Delivery, return and warranty policy text.
- The brand's guidance on depicting riding and safety gear.
- Whether displayed reviews come from a verified system.

## Check before you hand off

- The review was done on a phone-sized screen and on desktop.
- Every line has a recorded result.
- Specification mismatches are flagged as highest priority.
- No live change was made without approval.
- The facts sheet was updated if the page revealed new or changed facts.
