# 03 Store

> This chapter gives checklists for reviewing product pages and the trust and checkout experience, so that visitors can find accurate answers and buy without friction.

## Why this chapter exists

Every ad, video and chat conversation eventually sends a person to the store. If the page they land on is unclear, slow on a phone, missing the answer to an obvious question or inconsistent with what the ad said, the earlier work is wasted. An e-bike is a considered purchase: buyers look for specifics, compare, and need reassurance about delivery, assembly, returns and support before paying. The store is also the source of truth for every other agent, so errors here spread into ads and replies.

## Notes in this chapter

- [[Product Page Checklist]] walks through a product page from first screen to add-to-cart, covering clarity, accuracy, media, specifications, questions and mobile use.
- [[Store Trust And Checkout Audit]] covers the rest of the path: policies, contact details, reviews, cart, checkout steps and post-purchase messages.

## How to use it

1. Open the live store on a phone-sized screen first, then desktop.
2. Run the checklist and record each line as pass, fail or not applicable, with a screenshot or link for fails.
3. Report findings; do not edit the live store without explicit authorisation for each change.
4. After approved changes, re-check and update the [[Store Facts Sheet]].

## Guardrails for agents

- Audit and propose; publishing changes requires the store owner's instruction.
- Do not write or alter specifications, prices or policy text from your own knowledge. See [[Verified Claims Policy]].
- Do not create, edit or remove customer reviews.
- Place a real order only if the owner asks for a test purchase and explains how it will be handled.
