# 00 ACX Start Here

> This workspace holds process playbooks for agents working on an e-bike e-commerce brand, and it contains no brand facts, only how to do the work and what to verify first.

## What this workspace is

These notes describe procedures: how to structure an ad test, how to brief creative, how to audit a product page, how to reply to a lead. They were written without any knowledge of the specific brand. They do not contain product names, prices, specifications, warranty terms, locations or performance results, and an agent must not infer any from them.

## The two rules that override everything

1. **Facts come from the store, not from memory.** Before writing anything customer-facing, look up the relevant facts in the live store or approved brand documents and record where each came from. See [[Store Facts Sheet]].
2. **No unverified claims about battery range, speed, safety or legality.** These are the claims most likely to mislead a buyer or create liability. See [[Verified Claims Policy]].

## Notes in this chapter

- [[Verified Claims Policy]] defines which statements need a source, what counts as a source, and what to write when one is missing.
- [[Store Facts Sheet]] is the template for collecting and dating the brand facts every other playbook depends on.

## How to work

- Read the index of the chapter for your task, then only the notes you need.
- Fill the "Facts to confirm from the store" section of each note before producing output.
- If a fact cannot be confirmed, leave a clearly marked gap and ask a human; never fill it with a plausible guess.
- In every hand-off, list the sources used and anything left unverified.
