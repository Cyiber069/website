# Store Facts Sheet

> Before any customer-facing work, collect the brand's facts from the live store into one dated sheet so every agent quotes the same verified information.

## Why keep a sheet

Agents working separately will otherwise each look up, or guess, the same details and produce conflicting ads, pages and replies. One sheet with sources and dates makes facts consistent, shows what is still unknown, and makes stale information visible. It is the working companion to the [[Verified Claims Policy]].

## How to build it

1. Open the live store and policy pages. Do not rely on memory or older drafts.
2. For each item in the template, record the value, the page it came from and the date checked.
3. Where the store does not say, write "not published" and add it to open questions.
4. Ask a responsible human to answer open questions and to confirm the sheet.
5. Re-check anything time-sensitive, such as price, stock and promotions, at the moment you use it.

## Template

```text
STORE FACTS SHEET
Last full check: [date]   Checked by: [name or agent]

BUSINESS
- Brand name as written by the brand:
- Markets and languages served:
- Contact channels and hours:
- Physical locations or test-ride options, if any:

PRODUCTS (repeat per model)
- Model name exactly as on the store:
- Intended use as described by the store:
- Published specifications, copied exactly, with any conditions:
- Variants and what differs:
- Current price and currency:                  [checked: date]
- Stock or lead time:                          [checked: date]
- What is included in the box:
- Assembly required and how it is supported:

POLICIES
- Delivery areas, cost and time:
- Returns and cancellations:
- Warranty coverage and how to claim:
- Payment methods and instalment options:
- After-sales service and spare parts:

APPROVED WORDING
- Range, speed, safety and legal statements approved for use:
- Words or claims the brand has said to avoid:

OPEN QUESTIONS
- [question] - asked of [person] on [date]
```

## Rules for using it

- Copy specifications exactly, including units and conditions. Why: paraphrasing a figure changes its meaning.
- Never fill a blank by inference from a similar model.
- Record the source page for each fact so another agent can re-check it.
- If the store and a brand document disagree, flag the conflict and ask; do not choose.
- Start every task in [[02 Ads]], [[03 Store]], [[04 Content]] and [[05 Customer Messaging]] by reading the sheet.

## Facts to confirm from the store

- Everything in the template above.
- Who owns the sheet and approves changes.
- How often it should be fully re-checked.

## Check before you hand off

- Each entry has a source and a date.
- Blanks are marked "not published", not guessed.
- Time-sensitive items were re-checked at time of use.
- Open questions are listed with who was asked.
