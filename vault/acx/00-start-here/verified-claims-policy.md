# Verified Claims Policy

> Every factual statement about the products or the business must trace to a current source in the store or approved brand documents, and claims about range, speed, safety or legality need extra care.

## Why this policy exists

E-bike buyers make decisions on facts that vary by model, rider, terrain and local law. A wrong statement about how far a bike goes, how fast it assists, whether it is safe for a use, or whether it is legal to ride somewhere can cause financial loss, injury or legal trouble for the customer, and liability for the brand. Agents write quickly and fluently, which makes confident errors more dangerous, not less.

## Claim categories

| Category | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Battery range | State only the figure published by the store, with the store's own conditions and wording. Never estimate for a customer's weight, route or weather. |
| Speed and power | Quote published figures only. Never suggest ways to exceed limits. |
| Safety | No absolute words such as "safe", "safest" or "risk-free". Describe published features and point to the manual. |
| Legality | Never state that a bike is legal to ride in a place or without a licence. Say that rules vary by location and the customer should check local regulations, unless the brand has provided approved wording. |
| Price, stock, delivery, warranty, returns | Copy from the live store or policy pages at the time of writing. |

## What counts as a source

- The live product page or policy page, with the date checked.
- A document supplied by the brand owner, such as a specification sheet or manual.
- A written answer from a responsible human at the brand.

## When a source is missing

1. Do not write the claim.
2. Use neutral wording that promises a follow-up, for example: "Let me confirm that for you."
3. Add the question to the open items in the [[Store Facts Sheet]].
4. Escalate if the customer is waiting; see [[Handoff And Escalation Rules]].

## Facts to confirm from the store

- Published range, speed and power figures for each model, with any stated test conditions.
- Approved safety and legal wording, if any exists.
- Current warranty, return and delivery policy text.
- Who at the brand approves new claims.

## Check before you hand off

- Every factual claim in your output has a source and date noted.
- No statement about range, speed, safety or legality goes beyond published wording.
- No absolute or superlative claim is present without approved evidence.
- Unverified items are listed as open questions, not written as facts.
