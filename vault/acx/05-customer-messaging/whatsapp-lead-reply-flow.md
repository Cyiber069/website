# WhatsApp Lead Reply Flow

> Answer each new chat quickly, find out what the person needs before recommending anything, give verified answers, and end every exchange with one clear next step.

## Why a flow

Chat feels informal, but a lead's questions follow a predictable path. A defined flow keeps replies fast and consistent between agents, stops conversations stalling after the first answer, and makes sure facts are checked before they are sent.

## Stages

**1. Acknowledge.** Reply promptly within the brand's stated hours. Greet in the brand's voice, thank them, and answer their question directly if it is simple and verified. Why: a question answered with a question feels like a script.

**2. Understand.** Ask one or two short questions about their need, one at a time: what they will use the bike for, who it is for, anything that matters most to them. Why: recommendations without context are guesses.

**3. Answer and recommend.** Match their need to what the store actually sells, using the [[Store Facts Sheet]]. Share the product link. Quote specifications exactly as published.

**4. Handle hesitation.** Use the [[Objection Handling Approach]].

**5. Next step.** Offer one concrete action that the brand supports: the product link, a checkout link, a call or a visit if the brand offers them. Ask permission before following up.

**6. Follow up.** Only if they agreed, at the interval the brand sets, with something useful rather than a nudge. Stop when asked.

**7. Close and tag.** Record the outcome.

## Message patterns

```text
First reply:   Hi [name], thanks for your message. [Direct answer if verified.]
               So I can point you to the right option: [one question]?

Unknown fact:  Good question. I want to give you the exact answer, so let me
               confirm that and come back to you.

Range, speed, legality:
               The published figure for this model is on the product page:
               [link]. Actual results depend on conditions, and riding rules
               vary by location, so please check what applies where you ride.
               [Use the brand's approved wording where it exists.]

Next step:     Would you like the link to order, or is there anything else
               you would like to know first?
```

## Rules

- Never state a price, stock level, delivery time or policy without checking it at that moment.
- Never estimate range for the customer's weight, route or weather, and never advise on modifications.
- Do not request sensitive data in chat; payment happens through the store's checkout.
- Escalate per [[Handoff And Escalation Rules]].

## Tags

Use a small fixed set, for example: new, needs information, recommended, waiting on customer, handed to human, ordered, not a fit, opted out. Add the source if known. Why: tags let [[Weekly Ads Reporting]] judge lead quality and show where chats stall.

## Facts to confirm from the store

- Support hours and promised response time.
- Current products, prices, stock and delivery details.
- Next steps the brand actually offers, such as calls, visits or test rides.
- Approved wording for range, speed and legal questions.
- Follow-up rules and who handles handoffs.

## Check before you hand off

- Each reply's facts were checked against the sheet or store at time of sending.
- No message contains an unverified claim or an estimate.
- The conversation ends with a next step or a clear close.
- Tags are applied.
- Follow-up was sent only with permission.
