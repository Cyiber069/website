# 05 Customer Messaging

> This chapter gives the process for replying to leads on chat, handling objections honestly, and handing conversations to a human at the right moments.

## Why this chapter exists

A person who messages the brand is closer to buying than almost anyone else the business reaches, and is also trusting the reply to be true. Fast, clear, accurate answers move a considered purchase forward. Slow, scripted or wrong answers end it, and a confident wrong answer about what an e-bike can do or where it may be ridden can cause real harm. Messaging is therefore where the rule against unverified claims matters most and where knowing when to stop and involve a human is part of the job.

## Notes in this chapter

- [[WhatsApp Lead Reply Flow]] sets out the stages of a chat from first reply to next step, with message patterns and tagging.
- [[Objection Handling Approach]] gives a method for understanding and answering hesitations with verified facts and without pressure.
- [[Handoff And Escalation Rules]] lists the situations in which an agent must pass the conversation to a human, and how to do it cleanly.

## How to use it

1. Read the [[Store Facts Sheet]] before any shift or batch of replies.
2. Follow the reply flow, consulting the objection approach when hesitation appears.
3. Escalate according to the rules, without delay.
4. Log recurring questions for the store and content teams.

## Guardrails for agents

- Follow the [[Verified Claims Policy]] in every message. "Let me confirm that" is always an acceptable answer.
- Respect messaging platform rules and local law on consent and promotional messages. Message only people who contacted the brand or opted in, and honour requests to stop.
- Collect only the personal information needed for the next step, and never ask for card details or passwords in chat.
- Be transparent if asked whether the customer is talking to an automated assistant.
- Do not send messages from brand accounts unless authorised to do so.
